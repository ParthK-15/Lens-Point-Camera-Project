Canon EOS 7D Mark II

₹1,28,990/-

The Canon EOS 7D Mark II is a professional APS-C DSLR engineered for sports, wildlife, and action photography. It features a 20.2 MP sensor, 65-point autofocus system, 10 fps continuous shooting, and robust weather sealing for demanding environments.

Key Features

  • 20.2 MP APS-C CMOS sensor optimized for speed and accuracy.
  • 65-point all-cross autofocus system with intelligent tracking.
  • 10 fps continuous shooting for fast-moving subjects.
  • DIGIC 6 image processor for exceptional speed and performance.
  • Dual SD/UHS-II card slots for backup and fast recording.
  • Robust magnesium alloy body with comprehensive weather sealing.
