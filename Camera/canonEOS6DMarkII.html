Canon EOS 6D Mark II

₹1,94,995/-

The Canon EOS 6D Mark II is a full-frame DSLR designed for professional photographers and enthusiasts. It features a 26.2 MP full-frame sensor, 45-point autofocus system, 4K video recording, and built-in Wi-Fi and Bluetooth connectivity for seamless workflow.

Key Features

  • 26.2 MP Full-Frame CMOS sensor for ultra-high-resolution images.
  • 45-point autofocus system with 39 cross-type sensors for precision focusing.
  • DIGIC 7 image processor for excellent performance and low-light capability.
  • 4K UHD (24/25p) video recording with H.264 codec.
  • Built-in GPS, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth for connectivity and geotagging.
  • Articulating touchscreen LCD for flexible shooting angles.

Specifications
