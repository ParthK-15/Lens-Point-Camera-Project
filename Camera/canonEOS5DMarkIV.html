Canon EOS 5D Mark IV

₹3,08,758/-

The Canon EOS 5D Mark IV is a high-end full-frame DSLR designed for professional photographers and videographers. It features a 30.4 MP full-frame sensor, advanced autofocus system, and 4K video recording, making it ideal for weddings, wildlife, and commercial photography.

Key Features

  • 30.4 MP Full-Frame CMOS sensor for exceptional detail.
  • DIGIC 6+ image processor for high-speed performance.
  • 61-point autofocus system with high precision.
  • 4K video recording (DCI 4K).
  • Dual Pixel CMOS AF for smooth live view focusing.
  • Built-in Wi-Fi, GPS, and NFC.

Specifications

Sensor 30.4 MP Full-Frame CMOS
ISO 100 – 32000 (expandable to 102400)
Autofocus 61-point AF
Video 4K DCI (30fps), Full HD
Display 3.2-inch Touchscreen LCD
Weight ~890g
