Canon EOS 1D X Mark III Body

₹4,23,792/-

The Canon EOS 1D X Mark III is a flagship professional DSLR camera from Canon. Designed for sports, wildlife, and photojournalism, it features a 20.1 MP full-frame sensor, ultra-fast continuous shooting, and advanced deep learning autofocus for unmatched performance in demanding conditions.

Key Features

  • 20.1 MP Full-Frame CMOS Sensor for professional image quality.
  • DIGIC X Image Processor for ultra-fast performance.
  • 16 fps (OVF) / 20 fps (Live View) continuous shooting.
  • 191-point autofocus system with deep learning technology.
  • 5.5K RAW and 4K 60fps video recording.
  • ISO range of 100–102400 (expandable up to 819200).

High-Performance Full-Frame Sensor

The 20.1 MP full-frame CMOS sensor delivers exceptional image quality with excellent dynamic range and low-light performance, making it ideal for professional sports, wildlife, and event photography.

AI-Powered Autofocus

The 191-point autofocus system uses deep learning technology to track faces, eyes, and heads with incredible accuracy, ensuring sharp focus even in fast-paced action scenes.

Built for Fast Workflow

With built-in Ethernet, Wi-Fi, and USB-C connectivity, photographers can instantly transfer files, enabling real-time delivery for news and sports professionals.

Design

Rugged Professional Design

The Canon EOS 1D X Mark III features a durable magnesium alloy body with full weather sealing, built to withstand harsh environments. Its integrated vertical grip provides excellent handling during long shooting sessions.

Pro-Level Controls

The camera offers customizable buttons, dual card slots, and an intuitive interface designed for professional photographers who require speed, efficiency, and reliability.
