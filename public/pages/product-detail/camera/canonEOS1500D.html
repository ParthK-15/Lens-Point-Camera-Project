Canon EOS 1500D

₹43,990/-

The Canon EOS 1500D is an entry-level DSLR camera designed for beginners. It offers a 24.1 MP APS-C sensor, built-in Wi-Fi, and excellent image quality, making it perfect for photography enthusiasts stepping into DSLR photography.

Key Features

  • 24.1 MP APS-C CMOS Sensor for high-resolution images.
  • DIGIC 4+ Image Processor for improved performance.
  • 9-point autofocus system for precise focusing.
  • Full HD 1080p video recording.
  • Built-in Wi-Fi and NFC for easy sharing.
  • ISO range of 100–6400 (expandable up to 12800).

High-Resolution Sensor

The 24.1 MP APS-C CMOS sensor delivers detailed and sharp images, making it ideal for landscapes, portraits, and everyday photography.

Beginner-Friendly Autofocus

The 9-point autofocus system ensures accurate subject focusing, making it easy for beginners to capture clear and sharp photos.

Built-in Connectivity

With built-in Wi-Fi and NFC, users can quickly transfer photos to smartphones and share them on social media effortlessly.

Design

Lightweight & Comfortable Design

The Canon EOS 1500D features a compact and lightweight body, making it comfortable to carry for long shooting sessions. Its ergonomic grip ensures a secure hold for beginners.

Simple & Intuitive Controls

Designed for ease of use, the camera offers a simple interface and guided modes, helping new users learn photography quickly.
