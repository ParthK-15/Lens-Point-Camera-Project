Canon EOS 200D

₹55,695/-

The Canon EOS 200D is a compact and lightweight DSLR from Canon, designed for beginners and content creators. It features a 24.2 MP APS-C sensor, Dual Pixel autofocus, and a vari-angle touchscreen, making it perfect for photography and vlogging.

Key Features

  • 24.2 MP APS-C CMOS sensor for high-resolution images.
  • Dual Pixel CMOS autofocus for fast and smooth focusing.
  • DIGIC 7 image processor for improved performance.
  • Vari-angle touchscreen LCD for flexible shooting angles.
  • Full HD 1080p video recording with excellent clarity.
  • Built-in Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and NFC for easy sharing.
