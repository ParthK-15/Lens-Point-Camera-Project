Canon EOS 80D

₹79,999/-

The Canon EOS 80D is a versatile mid-range DSLR perfect for photography enthusiasts and semi-professionals. It features a 24.2 MP APS-C sensor, 45-point all-cross autofocus, 7 fps continuous shooting, articulating touchscreen, and 4K DCI video recording capability.

Key Features

  • 24.2 MP APS-C CMOS sensor for detailed, vibrant images.
  • 45-point all-cross autofocus system with intelligent tracking.
  • 7 fps continuous shooting for dynamic scene capture.
  • DIGIC 6 image processor for excellent color and performance.
  • Articulating 3.0-inch touchscreen for flexible composition.
  • 4K DCI video recording with Full HD slow motion options.

Specifications

Sensor 24.2 MP APS-C CMOS
ISO 100–16000 (expandable to 25600)
Autofocus 45-Point All-Cross AF System
Continuous Shooting 7 fps
Video 4K DCI, Full HD 1080p
Weight ~733g (body only)

Excellent APS-C Image Quality

The 24.2 MP APS-C CMOS sensor delivers sharp, detailed images with excellent color reproduction and dynamic range across a wide range of ISO settings.

45-Point All-Cross Autofocus

With 45 autofocus points including 39 cross-type sensors, the EOS 80D provides fast and accurate focus acquisition in diverse lighting and subject conditions.

4K DCI Video Recording

Capture professional-quality 4K DCI video alongside Full HD slow motion options, making it excellent for both stills photography and videography projects.

Design

Ergonomic and Durable Design

The Canon EOS 80D features a magnesium-alloy body with a comfortable grip and intuitive button layout, designed for all-day shooting comfort and durability.

Articulating Touchscreen

The 3.0-inch fully articulating touchscreen LCD enables flexible composition from any angle, perfect for both photo and video work in challenging positions.
