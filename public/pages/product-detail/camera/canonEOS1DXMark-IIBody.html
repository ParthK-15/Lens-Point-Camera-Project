Canon EOS 1D X Mark-II Body

₹4,23,792/-

The Canon EOS 1D X Mark II is a high-performance professional DSLR from Canon, designed for sports, wildlife, and action photography. It features a 20.2 MP full-frame sensor, fast continuous shooting, and advanced autofocus, making it a reliable choice for professionals.

Key Features

  • 20.2 MP full-frame CMOS sensor for high-quality professional images.
  • Dual DIGIC 6+ processors for fast performance and image processing.
  • 61-point high-density autofocus system for precise subject tracking.
  • 14 fps continuous shooting (up to 16 fps in Live View).
  • 4K video recording at 60fps for professional videography.
  • ISO range of 100–51200 (expandable up to 409600) for low-light shooting.

The 20.2 MP full-frame CMOS sensor delivers excellent image quality with rich detail and superior low-light performance, making it ideal for professional photography in challenging conditions.
