Canon EOS 3000D EF-S

₹35,955/-

The Canon EOS 3000D is an entry-level DSLR designed for beginners who want to step into photography. It features an 18 MP APS-C sensor, EF-S lens compatibility, and built-in Wi-Fi for easy sharing, making it a great budget-friendly choice.

Key Features

  • 18 MP APS-C CMOS sensor for decent image quality.
  • DIGIC 4+ image processor.
  • 9-point autofocus system.
  • Full HD 1080p video recording.
  • Compatible with Canon EF-S lenses.
  • Built-in Wi-Fi for instant sharing.

Specifications

Sensor 18 MP APS-C CMOS
ISO 100 – 6400 (expandable to 12800)
Autofocus 9-point AF
Video Full HD 1080p
Display 2.7-inch LCD
Weight ~436g
