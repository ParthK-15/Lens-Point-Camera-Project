Boya BM-2021 Microphone

₹7,200/-

The Boya BM-2021 is a compact supercardioid shotgun microphone made for DSLR and mirrorless creators who need focused, cleaner voice pickup. Its lightweight build and shock-mounted design make it ideal for vlogs, interviews, and run-and-gun video production.

Key Features

  • Supercardioid polar pattern for directional sound capture.
  • Integrated shock mount reduces handling and vibration noise.
  • Compact hot-shoe mount design for on-camera workflows.
  • Foam windshield support for outdoor recording stability.
  • Lightweight body that balances well with mirrorless cameras.
  • Plug-and-record usage for content creators and vloggers.

Specifications

Type Shotgun Condenser
Polar Pattern Supercardioid
Frequency Range 40Hz - 20kHz
Sensitivity -38 dB
Output 3.5mm TRS
Weight Approx. 63 g

Camera-Ready Setup

The microphone sits securely on the hot shoe and maintains stability while panning, making it suitable for everyday handheld video shoots.

Focused Voice Pickup

Portable Creator Kit

A portable-friendly form factor with accessory compatibility helps creators build a practical audio kit for travel, weddings, and documentary-style content.
